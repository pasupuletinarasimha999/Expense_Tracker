Privacy Policy — Expense Tracker

Last updated: August 30, 2026

Expense Tracker is a personal budgeting app. This policy explains what data the app handles and why.

Data stored on your device

All transactions, categories, trips, and budgets you enter are stored locally on your device in a private SQLite database. This data is never sent to us or to any third party, and we have no server that receives it.

Google Sign-In & Drive backup (optional)

If you choose to sign in with Google, the app requests access to a single, narrow scope:

  • drive.appdata — the Drive "Application Data" folder, a hidden storage space tied only to this app. It is not your visible Drive files, and no other app (including Google Drive's own UI) can browse it.

This is used solely to back up and restore your own transactions and settings across your devices. We do not read, collect, or have any access to this data ourselves — it moves directly between your device and your own Google Drive storage.

Signing in also shares your basic Google account profile (name, email, avatar) with the app, used only to show which account is currently backed up.

Biometric / PIN app lock

If enabled, biometric authentication (Face ID / fingerprint) is handled entirely by your device's operating system. The app never receives or stores your biometric data — only a yes/no confirmation that you unlocked it.

Notifications

Local reminders (e.g. trip expense due dates) are scheduled and delivered entirely on-device. No data leaves your phone to generate these.

Exporting reports

CSV/PDF export files are generated on your device and shared only through the share sheet you choose (e.g. Mail, Gmail, Files) — the app itself does not transmit them anywhere.

What we don't do

  • No analytics or usage tracking
  • No advertising or ad networks
  • No third-party data sharing or selling
  • No account or server of our own — your data is never sent to us

Data deletion

Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data. To remove your Drive backup, disconnect the app from your Google Account at myaccount.google.com/permissions, which deletes the associated appdata folder.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will be revised.
